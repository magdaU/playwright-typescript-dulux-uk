## Open items

- [ ] **Monitor `BUG-005` (tester out of stock site-wide) and re-run the purchase journey once resolved.** See
      [BUG_REPORTS.md](BUG_REPORTS.md#bug-005--buy-a-tester-is-unavailable-site-wide-the-tester-product-cant-be-ordered-online)
      for the full repro. `tests/specs/purchase/tester-product.spec.ts` (`@purchase @regression @smoke`) is left
      failing deliberately — it's an honest signal, not a suite bug — but check back periodically (e.g. before
      relying on a green `@smoke` run) and close this out once "Buy a Tester in this colour" is visible again on
      a shade-detail page.
- [ ] **Cookie-consent banner intermittently reappears despite `storageState`.** First seen while building
      `tests/specs/search/colour-search.spec.ts`: a run against production failed with the OneTrust consent
      overlay (`onetrust-pc-dark-filter`) blocking a click that should've been well past consent (storage state
      is regenerated by `tests/setup/global-setup.ts` before every run). A retry with identical setup passed
      cleanly. **Seen again** while building `tests/specs/visualizer/visualizer-app.spec.ts` — both desktop and
      mobile runs failed on the same overlay on the first attempt, then passed cleanly on retry. **Seen
      repeatedly again** while investigating `BUG-005` (several exploratory runs against the colour-detail and
      product pages hit it, always recovering on retry). It affects any spec that relies on the shared
      `storageState` (i.e. the whole suite), and is frequent enough now that it's worth prioritising over the
      other open items here.
      **Why not fixed now:** each occurrence was out of scope for the PR that hit it; needs its own investigation
      rather than a silent workaround bolted onto individual specs.
      **Suggested next step:** add a shared, defensive "dismiss consent if present" helper (e.g. on `BasePage`
      or as a fixture-level `beforeEach`) so any spec recovers instead of failing outright — then decide whether
      that's enough, or whether `global-setup.ts` itself needs a retry/verification step before persisting
      `storageState`.
- [ ] Root-cause the Firefox/WebKit divergence in the purchase journey (see

- [ ] Promote `@a11y` into `@regression` once the violations in [BUG_REPORTS.md](BUG_REPORTS.md) are fixed
